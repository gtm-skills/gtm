# Cold Email Examples by Industry

Each follows observation → relevance → ask. Word counts in brackets. Replace specifics with real ones; never send an invented fact.

## SaaS — VP Sales

Subject: new pricing page

You moved from three tiers to usage-based pricing on Tuesday.
Reps usually struggle for a quarter to forecast usage deals without a new qualification step.
Has forecast accuracy come up since the switch? [37]

## FinTech — Head of Compliance

Subject: the SOC 2 badge

Your trust page added SOC 2 Type II last month.
The next question enterprise buyers ask is how fast you can turn security questionnaires.
Is questionnaire turnaround a metric you track? [34]

## Healthcare Tech — VP Customer Success

Subject: 40 new hospital logos

Your Q3 release listed forty new hospital systems.
Onboarding that many at once usually means implementation managers doing training by hand.
Are you adding implementation headcount or changing the process? [36]

## Manufacturing — Director of Operations

Subject: the Monterrey plant

You announced the Monterrey plant for Q1.
Second-site launches tend to double the time spent reconciling supplier data between systems.
Is supplier data something your team is cleaning up ahead of the launch? [37]

## Professional Services — Managing Partner

Subject: your partner promotions

You promoted six to partner in January.
New partners usually inherit a book without a repeatable way to find expansion in it.
Is account growth part of their first-year plan? [33]

## E-commerce — VP Marketing

Subject: the Klaviyo move

Your emails switched to Klaviyo two weeks ago.
Migrations like that usually leave a gap in post-purchase flows for a few months.
Is post-purchase on the roadmap this quarter? [32]

## Real Estate — Brokerage Owner

Subject: 12 new agents

You added twelve agents to the Westside office this spring.
New agents usually spend their first ninety days without a consistent follow-up system.
Is agent ramp something you measure? [31]

## Education — Dean of Admissions

Subject: fall deposit deadline

Your deposit deadline moved to May 1.
Earlier deadlines tend to push more yield work into a shorter window for admissions counselors.
Is yield this cycle tracking where you want it? [33]

## Logistics — VP Operations

Subject: the Dallas cross-dock

You opened a cross-dock in Dallas in March.
New nodes usually expose which carrier data is still coming in by email.
Is carrier visibility a project this year? [30]

## Insurance — Head of Distribution

Subject: independent agent channel

You said in the Q2 letter that independent agents are the growth channel.
Agencies usually rank carriers by how fast they can get a quote back.
Is quote turnaround a number you watch? [35]

## Government Contractor — Capture Manager

Subject: the GSA schedule

Your GSA MAS award went through in February.
Schedule holders usually see RFQ volume jump before capture capacity does.
Are you adding capture capacity or triaging harder? [32]

## Founder — any

Subject: your launch post

You wrote on Tuesday that the first ten customers all came from your network.
Founders at that stage usually hit a wall around customer fifteen when the network runs out.
Is outbound something you are starting, or still deferring? [40]
